## What would flip a no

- A big one-time discount on the yearly plan, like $15 for the year, or a launch promo I could grab before my exam.
- A few people in the IELTS Telegram group saying it really helped their Speaking band, and the free tier working well on my phone in a noisy place.
- Several people in the IELTS or job-search Telegram groups saying it really helped them in an actual interview, with screenshots of their scores improving.
- If people in my IELTS Telegram chats said their real Speaking band went up after using it, plus a one-time price of about $10 for the 2–3 months until my exam instead of a subscription.
- If the free 3 fragments actually caught mistakes my teacher missed and worked with my mic without crashing, I'd pay for one month of Pro right before the exam, as long as I'm not forced into a trial.
- If it works without problems on the free tier for a week and the scores actually match my mistakes, and I can pay for just one month with no auto-renewal before my interview, then I'd take a month at $4.99.
- My tutor or people in the IELTS Telegram chats recommending it and saying it really fixed their accent before an interview.
- If a week of the free trial showed my scores going up in a way ChatGPT can't show me, and there was a one-time price around $10 for the months until my exam instead of a subscription.
- If people in my IELTS Telegram chats showed that the scores matched their real speaking results, and there was a one-time cheap pass for the 1–2 months until my interview instead of a subscription.
- If during the trial it really caught my mistakes accurately even with background noise, and then there was a discount on the yearly plan, around $19.99.
- My tutor recommending it, or her checking a few of my recordings and agreeing with the app's scores.
- If my tutor told me to use it for homework between lessons.
