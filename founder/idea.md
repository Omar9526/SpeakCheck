# SpeakCheck

- What it is: an iOS app where you read your own English text aloud and get a 0–100 pronunciation score for every word and sound, with explanations of mistakes in Russian.
- Who it is for: Russian-speaking adults in the CIS (starting with Kyrgyzstan) who already read English but are not confident speaking it — students preparing for IELTS/interviews, IT and office workers who present or call in English, people preparing to relocate.
- What it sells, at what price: the core loop (upload your PDF/Word/photo → read a fragment aloud → per-word and per-sound score from Microsoft Azure Pronunciation Assessment → Russian explanation from DeepSeek → retry the hard word). **Price is not decided yet.** Working hypothesis to test (not validated): free with a daily limit of fragments, plus a "Pro" subscription for unlimited practice; the price level is an open question.
- Where and how: online, iPhone only (iOS 17+), App Store in CIS countries. Growth is planned through built-in sharing: a result card for Instagram/Telegram stories, a "challenge a friend" link (friend reads the same text, scores are compared), a daily streak and a word of the day. Guest mode — usable without registration; Sign in with Apple optional.
- Budget and constraints: solo founder, no marketing budget stated. Product is already built (iOS app + Django backend, tests passing), not yet deployed: needs a server, Azure Speech key, DeepSeek key and an Apple Developer account. Variable cost per use: Azure Speech per audio minute and DeepSeek per explanation (exact unit cost not yet calculated). Scores are a learning guide, not a certified CEFR/IELTS test.
