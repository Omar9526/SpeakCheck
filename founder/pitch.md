SpeakCheck — an iPhone app to prepare for IELTS Speaking and job interviews in English.
Read an answer aloud (built-in IELTS Part 1/2/3 and interview answers, or your own text from PDF, Word or a photo) and get a 0–100 score for every word and every sound, with an explanation of each mistake in Russian.
Set your exam or interview date: the app shows a countdown and a short task list for each day.
Free: 3 fragments a day. Pro: unlimited practice for $4.99 a month or $29.99 a year, paid through the App Store; the first 7 days of Pro are free.
It trains pronunciation only; it does not grade vocabulary or grammar, and the score is not an official IELTS result.
